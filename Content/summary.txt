 Core Logic:
1. Initial Trade Execution:

Open 1 Long with the broker offering the lowest Ask.

Open 1 Short with the broker offering the highest Bid.

Lot size is user-defined (e.g., 0.01).

2. Continuous Monitoring & Replacement:

EA monitors all brokers in real-time.

Replace Long if a new broker has Ask < current Long Bid by X points.

Replace Short if a new broker has Bid > current Short Ask by X points.

After replacement, wait X seconds before the next search (user-defined).

3. Broker Disconnection Handling:

Pause all trading if any broker disconnects.

Show “Stopped” (Red) until manually restarted.

Don’t close trades, just halt operations.

4. Start/Stop Button:

“Started” (Green) when active.

“Stopped” (Red) on manual stop or disconnection.
